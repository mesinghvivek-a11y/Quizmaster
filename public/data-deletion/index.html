Delete your data

You are in control. Here is how to remove your information from My PYQ Online.

1. Clear data stored on your device

In the app open Performance → Reset account to erase all progress, sessions and notes on that device. Open Subject → Add subject to remove subjects you imported. You can also clear your browser’s site data for www.mypyq.online.

2. Delete your account and synced data

Email support@mypyq.online from the email address of your Google account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify the request, delete your account profile (Google ID, email, name, picture) and synced study data within 30 days, and confirm by email. Backups are overwritten within a further 30 days.

3. Remove My PYQ Online’s access to your Google account

Visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose My PYQ Online and select Delete all connections. This stops future sign-ins with that connection; it does not by itself delete data we already hold — use step 2 for that.

What we may keep

Server logs (kept up to 90 days) and records we must retain by law or to resolve disputes. Everything else is deleted.

Parents and guardians

A parent or guardian can request deletion of a child’s data by emailing the same address with proof of relationship. See our Privacy Policy for details.

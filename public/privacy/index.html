1. Who we are

My PYQ Online (“My PYQ Online”, “we”, “us”) is a study platform at https://www.mypyq.online for practising previous-year questions (PYQs). The service is operated by [Your full name or registered business name], India. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have — including how we handle information we receive when you use Sign in with Google.

2. Summary

  • If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and a Google account ID — nothing else from your Google account.
  • We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service, and we never see your Google password.
  • We use that information only to create and recognise your account and to show your name and picture inside the app.
  • We do not sell your data, do not show advertising, and do not share Google data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Your study progress and notes are saved on your own device and, once you sign in, with your account so that they can follow you.
  • You can delete your data at any time — see how to delete your data.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information from Sign in with Google

When you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares the following with us (OAuth scopes openid, email and profile):

DataWhy we need it
Google account ID (a unique, non-public identifier)To recognise your account each time you return, without a password.
Email address and whether it is verifiedTo identify your account, send essential service messages (for example account deletion confirmations) and respond to support requests.
NameTo greet you and show your name in the app and, if you choose, in live quiz rooms.
Profile picture URLTo show your avatar in the app.

We do not request access to any other Google data or services. Signing in is optional where the app can be used on your device without an account.

3.2 Information you create in the app

  • Answers, scores, practice and test session history, time spent, daily goals and streaks.
  • Notes, flags and revision schedules for questions.
  • Subjects and questions you import from Excel, CSV or JSON files.
  • Preferences such as theme and text size, and the display name you use in live rooms.

3.3 Live quiz rooms

When you create or join a live room, your display name, the room code, your answers and your response times are shared with the other participants in that room. Live-room messages are carried by third-party message relay servers (see section 6). We do not keep a copy of room traffic; your own result is saved to your history.

3.4 Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure automatically records technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, pages requested and timestamps. We use it only to operate, secure and debug the service. We do not use advertising identifiers.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the service: sign you in, store and restore your progress, run practice and tests, schedule revision and host live quizzes.
  • To keep the service safe: prevent abuse, fraud and security incidents.
  • To communicate with you about the service, support requests and important changes.
  • To improve content and features using aggregated or de-identified statistics.
  • To comply with applicable law.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

5. Google user data and Limited Use

My PYQ Online’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • What we use it for: only to authenticate you, create and recognise your account, and display your name and picture to you.
  • No other uses: we do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or to train generalised AI or machine-learning models.
  • No transfers: we do not sell Google user data or transfer it to third parties, except to service providers acting on our instructions to operate the service, where required by law, or for security investigations.
  • No human access: our team does not read Google user data except with your consent, for security or abuse investigations, to comply with law, or when the data is aggregated and anonymised.
  • Revoking access: you can disconnect My PYQ Online at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers (processors) that host our website, database and authentication, and send service emails. They may process data only on our instructions and under confidentiality and security obligations. A list of providers is available on request at support@mypyq.online.
  • Other participants in a live room you join: your display name, answers and timing, as described in 3.3.
  • Public message relay servers. To connect friends on different devices, live-room messages pass through free public message brokers (EMQX, HiveMQ and Eclipse Mosquitto) over encrypted WebSocket connections. These relays are operated by third parties, and room messages are not end-to-end encrypted, so do not put sensitive information in a display name. Anyone who knows a room code could join that room while it is open.
  • Legal and safety. Where required by law, court order or government request, or to protect rights, safety and the integrity of the service.
  • Business transfers. If the service is transferred to another operator, your information may transfer with it, subject to this policy.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use only strictly necessary storage: your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB keep your progress, settings and imported subjects on your device, and (once sign-in is enabled) a session token keeps you signed in. We do not use advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies. Clearing your browser data removes the information stored on your device. If we later add analytics, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Account data (Google profile details and synced study data) is kept while your account is active.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle, normally within a further 30 days.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days.
  • On-device data stays until you reset the app or clear your browser data.
  • We may keep limited records where the law requires, or to resolve disputes and enforce our terms.

9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may: access the personal data we hold about you; correct inaccurate data; request erasure; withdraw your consent at any time; seek grievance redressal; and nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity. Within the app you can reset your progress (Performance → Reset account), export a backup, and remove imported subjects. To exercise any right, email support@mypyq.online from the address linked to your account. We will respond within 30 days. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out.

10. Children and students under 18

The service is designed for exam students, many of whom may be under 18. If you are under 18 you should use My PYQ Online, and sign in, only with the knowledge and consent of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from a child without that consent, we do not track or monitor children’s behaviour for advertising, and we do not target advertising at children. A parent or guardian may contact us at support@mypyq.online to review or delete a child’s data.

11. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and reasonable organisational safeguards to protect your data. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

12. International processing

Our service providers may process data on servers located outside India. Where this happens, we require appropriate safeguards and process data only as described in this policy.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new “Last updated” date and, for material changes, notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.

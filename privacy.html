We do not collect your phone number, address, payment details or government ID. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google service. If you never sign in, your study data stays only on your device.

2. How we use data

Only to provide the features above: sign-in, sync, reports, shared banks, live rooms, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your data, do not show advertising, and do not use your data for profiling or to train AI models.

3. Storage on your device

The app uses your browser's local storage, IndexedDB and a service-worker cache to remember progress, work offline and keep you signed in. These are strictly necessary for the service. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies and do not run analytics.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Google — sign-in (Google Identity).
  • Supabase — database, authentication and real-time messaging.
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr (CDN) — deliver fonts and a code library; they receive your IP address when your browser loads them.

These providers may process data on servers outside India. We disclose data to authorities only where the law requires.

5. Google user data (Limited Use)

My PYQ Online's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google account data is used only to sign you in and identify your account.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the footer link "Delete my account" on the home page (while signed in), or by emailing us. Deletion removes your profile, synced study data, reports and shared subjects from our database; any remaining copies in provider backups are overwritten in their normal cycle. Data stored only on your device can be cleared from the app's Reset option or your browser settings.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, where relevant, the GDPR), you may ask to access, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent (by signing out and deleting your account) and raise a grievance. Email support@mypyq.online; we aim to respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The service is for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and protected by access rules so each user can read only their own synced data. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users and authorities of a data breach as the law requires.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above; material changes will be highlighted on the home page.
